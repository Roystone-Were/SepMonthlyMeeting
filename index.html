01 · Where we started

Issues raised.

Seven items the team agreed to fix. This is the checklist we measure the rest of the deck against.

  • Delays on transfer orders to Katani.
  • Transfer orders to be dispatched through the receiving bay.
  • Kitchen sockets to be fixed on higher areas for easy accessibility.
  • Conduct stocktake for Syokimau.
  • Shelf labels for wholesale products to be fixed.
  • Get clear attire for fire and safety marshals.
  • Deli barcodes to be scanning.
