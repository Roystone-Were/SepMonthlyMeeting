01 · Where we started

Issues raised.

Six items the team agreed to fix. This is the checklist the rest of the deck is measured against.

  • Kitchen sockets to be fixed on higher areas for easy accessibility, and open sockets to be fixed.
  • Unavailability of products in the POS.
  • Back-up pharmacy cooler.
  • Regular maintenance of signages.
  • Regular servicing of deli appliances.
  • Thorough marketing of deli products and corporate onboarding.
02 · Progress

Issues closed.

Closed · Deli marketing

Done

Marketing of deli products is done every day.

Closed · Corporate onboarding

Done

Marketing and onboarding of corporates, SMEs and institutions complete; now converting leads into customers and closing deals.

Closed · Deli appliances

Done

Deli appliances are now serviced regularly to keep them in good working condition.

03 · Still open

Pending issues.

  1. Some products are physically on the shelf but unavailable in the POS, despite the stock take.
  2. Signages require regular maintenance.
  3. We still do not have a back-up cooler for the pharmacy.
  4. Kitchen sockets are yet to be fixed in accessible areas.

What we agree before leaving.

  3. Work on pricing from the stock controller department so that we can be more competitive.
  4. Shelve our wholesales for better visibility.
  5. Customer service.
